AI Solutions & Intelligent Automation

Most AI demos look great and fall apart on real data. We design for the real thing from day one: permission-aware access, evaluation before rollout, and cost and quality you can measure. Models, vector stores and cloud are chosen to fit your environment, not ours.

High-impact AI services

AI that earns its place.

Three areas where well-engineered AI pays off first.

Enterprise Knowledge Search (RAG)

Natural-language answers across your documents, wikis, tickets and code, grounded in your content with citations back to the source. Built with hybrid search, reranking and permission-aware retrieval, so people only see what they’re already allowed to see.

Document & Workflow Automation

Layout-aware parsing and schema-constrained extraction for invoices, contracts, forms and tickets. Each field carries a confidence score; low-confidence items go to a human review queue, and clean results flow into your ERP, CRM or ticketing system.

Data Readiness Audit

A hands-on assessment of data quality, sensitive-data exposure, pipelines and API readiness. You get a prioritized use-case list and a practical roadmap before any build budget is committed.

Responsible AI Framework

Intelligent, and accountable.

Trust is designed in at the architecture level, not added at the end.

Data Security

Your data is not used to train public models. We support private and in-tenant deployments, PII redaction, encryption, and audit logging.

  • Role-based, permission-aware access
  • Private deployment options

Traceability

Every answer cites its sources, and we test for groundedness, so unsupported answers are caught and reduced rather than hidden.

  • Source citations
  • Groundedness checks

Human Validation

Review steps sit where an error would be costly: approvals, escalations and low-confidence cases, with a full record of who decided what.

  • Human-in-the-loop
  • Decision audit trail

How we build

Engineered for real-world data.

A few principles guide every solution we design.

Evaluation first

We build a test set from your real examples before we build the solution, so quality is measured against facts instead of impressions.

Model-agnostic

We choose models and tools for your use case, budget and security needs, and design so you can switch later without a rebuild.

Hybrid retrieval

Keyword and semantic search combined with reranking, for answers that are accurate as well as fluent.

Permission-aware by design

Access rules from your existing systems carry through to the AI layer, so people only see what they’re already allowed to see.

Cloud of your choice

AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or your own environment, wherever your data and policies need it to be.

Built to be maintained

Monitoring, cost tracking, documentation and a clean handover, so your team can run and extend what we build.

The 9sprints engine
