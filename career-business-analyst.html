Business Analyst

Hyderabad, IN · Bangalore, IN

Apply for this roleBack to all roles

About the role

As a Business Analyst, you’ll dig into client problems, turn them into clear requirements and acceptance criteria, and keep everyone aligned as the solution takes shape. You’ll work with stakeholders, Product Owners and engineers to make sure we solve the right problem in the right way.

What you’ll do

  • Run discovery sessions and gather requirements from stakeholders
  • Document requirements, user stories, process flows and acceptance criteria
  • Keep clients and delivery teams aligned as the solution evolves
  • Support testing and user acceptance with clear scenarios

What we’re looking for

  • Experience as a Business Analyst on software or data projects
  • Strong analytical, writing and communication skills
  • Familiarity with agile delivery and tools such as Jira and Confluence
  • Interest in AI and automation use cases is a plus

What we offer

  • Work on real projects with a collaborative, supportive team
  • Room to learn, take ownership and grow with the company
  • Clear communication, sensible process and respect for your time
