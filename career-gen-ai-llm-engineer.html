Gen AI / LLM Engineer

San Francisco, CA · Bangalore, IN

Apply for this roleBack to all roles

About the role

As a Gen AI Engineer, you’ll build the working parts of our AI solutions, including RAG pipelines, extraction workflows, integrations and APIs. You’ll move ideas from prototype to production-ready systems, with testing, monitoring and clean documentation along the way.

What you’ll do

  • Build RAG pipelines, extraction workflows and LLM-powered applications
  • Integrate AI components with client systems through APIs
  • Write tests, add monitoring and document what you build
  • Help improve quality, speed and cost over time

What we’re looking for

  • Strong Python skills and experience building production software
  • Hands-on experience with LLM APIs, vector databases and orchestration frameworks
  • Understanding of retrieval, evaluation and prompt design
  • Comfort working in agile teams with code reviews and CI/CD

What we offer

  • Work on real projects with a collaborative, supportive team
  • Room to learn, take ownership and grow with the company
  • Clear communication, sensible process and respect for your time
