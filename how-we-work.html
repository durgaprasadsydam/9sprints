Why 9sprints?

Why 9sprints?

Our name comes from the sprint, the two-week cycle modern delivery teams work in. We plan a typical build, from first conversation to launch, at around nine of them.

Nine sprints. One team. Built with you.

Every sprint, four moments

Plan, build, review, reflect.

Every sprint follows the same simple rhythm.

Plan

We agree what we’re building and why.

Build

We design, build and test.

Review

You see it working and steer.

Reflect

We learn and improve the next sprint.
