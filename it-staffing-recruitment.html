Overview

Hiring, without the drag.

Hiring great engineers is slow, noisy and expensive. We make it fast and predictable: a deep, pre-vetted network, a technical assessment for every candidate, and a shortlist in your inbox within 48 hours. Whether you need one contractor next week or a full offshore team next quarter, we handle sourcing, screening, onboarding and compliance.
