How we work with you

From first conversation to launch, together.

The same clear path on every engagement, with you involved at each step and no surprises.

  1. Discover (1–2 weeks)

    We listen first. Together we map your goals, your users, your existing systems and what success looks like.

  2. Define (1–2 weeks)

    We turn ideas into a plan: architecture, a prototype, a prioritized backlog and a sprint roadmap you’ve approved.

  3. Deliver (2-week sprints)

    We build in short sprints. Every two weeks you see working software, a live demo and an honest status.

  4. Launch & Grow (2–4 weeks, then ongoing)

    We take it live with a planned, low-risk release, hand over full documentation and keep improving it with you.
